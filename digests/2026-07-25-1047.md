# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 25 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Sierra's aggressive acquisition strategy in long-horizon AI agents, coupled with Agentforce's continued rapid ARR growth and ServiceNow's strategic AI platform expansion, highlight the intensifying competition in agentic AI. Kore.ai must continue to emphasize its proven, enterprise-grade platform's deterministic execution and comprehensive analyst validation against these rapidly evolving, well-funded players.

## Watch for
- Continued integration and market positioning of Sierra's "Horizon" platform post-Takeoff acquisition.
- Salesforce's continued push of Agentforce into voice and broader CX use cases, especially in regulated industries.
- ServiceNow's ongoing consolidation of AI capabilities under the "Otto" umbrella and its impact on enterprise adoption.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven leadership in enterprise conversational AI for CX** in analyst briefings and sales enablement, highlighting our top-tier placements in Gartner and Forrester.
- Arm sales with specific talking points on the **predictability and cost-efficiency of Kore.ai's per-outcome pricing** versus the potential "credit anxiety" of consumption-based models like Salesforce's Agentforce.
- Develop messaging that contrasts Kore.ai's **purpose-built, unified CX platform** with the "stitched-together" approach of acquired capabilities, particularly relevant for NICE/Cognigy and ServiceNow's evolving AI stacks.
- Continue to stress the **deterministic execution and compile-time validation** of Kore.ai's ABL as a critical differentiator for complex, long-horizon agentic workflows, countering the inherent risks of purely LLM-driven approaches.
