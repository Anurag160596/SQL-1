# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 26 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Sierra's acquisition of Takeoff signals a strategic push into "long-horizon" agent capabilities, directly challenging Kore.ai's platform breadth. Simultaneously, Genesys and NICE/Cognigy are reinforcing their CCaaS-centric AI plays, highlighting the ongoing battle between specialized AI platforms and incumbent giants.

### Sierra Builds Long-Horizon Agent Platform

### Sierra Acquires Takeoff for Long-Horizon AI Agents
**What happened —** Sierra announced its acquisition of Takeoff, a company focused on long-horizon AI agents, to build out its "Horizon" platform. This move aims to expand Sierra's capabilities beyond immediate task completion to more complex, multi-step agentic workflows. **What it means for Kore.ai —** This acquisition directly targets the "long-horizon" agentic AI space, a key area of differentiation for Kore.ai's ABL. We must emphasize our compile-time validation and deterministic execution for complex, multi-step processes, contrasting with potential LLM-driven unpredictability in Sierra's new offering.

## Watch for
*   Further details on Sierra's "Horizon" platform capabilities and early customer adoption.
*   Impact of the Genesys-AWS partnership on Genesys's AI agent roadmap and competitive positioning against specialized platforms.
*   Salesforce's continued efforts to simplify Agentforce pricing and demonstrate clear ROI beyond ARR growth.

*   **Messaging:** Emphasize Kore.ai's leadership in "long-horizon" agentic AI and our proven deterministic execution for complex, multi-step processes.
*   **Sales Enablement:** Arm sales with talking points highlighting the TCO predictability and platform independence of Kore.ai versus the consumption-based pricing and ecosystem lock-in of competitors like Salesforce Agentforce.
*   **Product Marketing:** Develop collateral that clearly articulates the benefits of our provider-agnostic model and deep integration capabilities, contrasting with CCaaS incumbents leveraging hyperscaler AI.
*   **Analyst Relations:** Continue to reinforce our Leader position in CX-specific conversational AI evaluations, particularly against competitors who are not recognized in these key analyst reports.
