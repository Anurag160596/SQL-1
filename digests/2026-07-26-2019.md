# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 26 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing aggressive AI agent capabilities and strategic partnerships, but gaps in true omnichannel, predictable TCO, and independent analyst validation persist, creating clear openings for Kore.ai's proven, enterprise-grade platform.

### Sierra Acquires Takeoff, Bolstering Agent Platform

**What happened —** Sierra announced its acquisition of Takeoff on (2026-07-24) to enhance its "long-horizon AI agents" and build out its "Horizon" platform, as reported by CMSWire and Pulse 2.0.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This move signals Sierra's intent to deepen its agentic capabilities, but Kore.ai's ABL provides compile-time validation and deterministic execution, which are critical for enterprise reliability that Sierra's "long-horizon" approach may not yet guarantee.

### Genesys and AWS Partner to Redefine AI-Driven Engagement

**What happened —** Genesys and AWS are collaborating to redefine AI-driven customer engagement, according to a report by The Futurum Group on (2026-07-24).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While this partnership highlights the trend of CCaaS incumbents leveraging cloud providers, Kore.ai's AI-native platform and its proven leadership in independent conversational AI evaluations (Gartner MQ, Forrester Wave) offer a more robust, purpose-built solution than bolted-on AI from a CCaaS vendor.

### NICE Expands RingCentral Partnership with AI Focus

**What happened —** NICE is expanding its strategic partnership with RingCentral, with a particular emphasis on AI-powered customer and employee experiences, as detailed by Business Wire on (2026-07-23).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This partnership underscores the market's move towards integrated AI solutions. Kore.ai's single, unified platform and its established leadership in conversational AI evaluations provide a more cohesive and validated approach compared to stitching together capabilities through partnerships.

## Watch for
- Continued integration of Moveworks into ServiceNow's Otto platform.
- Any further analyst evaluations that position competitors as leaders in CX conversational AI.
- New pricing models or consumption challenges from Salesforce's Agentforce.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven leadership** in independent analyst evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) for conversational AI in customer service, contrasting with competitors' narrower or absent CX-specific validation.
- Highlight the **predictability and auditability** of Kore.ai's ABL and compile-time validation against competitors' "long-horizon" or consumption-based AI models that may lack deterministic execution and TCO certainty.
- Position Kore.ai as the **true omnichannel platform** against voice-first or digitally-focused solutions, underscoring our ability to handle both voice and digital interactions seamlessly within a single, unified stack.
- Arm sales with specific questions around **cost-per-resolution** and **deterministic behavior** for regulated industries when engaging with competitors like Sierra, Genesys, and NICE.
- Leverage ServiceNow's security incident to reinforce Kore.ai's platform **reliability and governance** strengths.
