# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 27 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, with Salesforce and ServiceNow highlighting their platform-native approaches and Genesys and NICE (Cognigy) leveraging their existing CCaaS dominance. Kore.ai's strength lies in its proven, independent platform leadership and its ability to offer a unified, governed, and truly omnichannel solution that avoids the integration complexities and potential lock-in of these larger players.

## Three signals that matter

### Salesforce Agentforce Drives ARR with Broad Platform Push
**What happened —** Salesforce reported Agentforce ARR of $1.2B, a 205% YoY increase, driven by a broad platform push including new versions and pricing models. Salesforce IR (2026-07-27)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While impressive ARR growth, Salesforce's pricing model remains complex and prone to customer anxiety, a key opening for Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and clear TCO.

### ServiceNow Unifies Agentic AI Under "Otto"
**What happened —** ServiceNow is consolidating its AI agent capabilities under the "Otto" umbrella, integrating Moveworks and its own Now Assist offerings, and has introduced new pricing tiers for custom agents. ServiceNow (2026-07-24)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** ServiceNow's ITSM-centric approach and the "assists" consumption model present TCO unpredictability, a stark contrast to Kore.ai's governed, deterministic platform that avoids credit-gobbling retry loops and offers clear cost attribution.

### Genesys Enhances CCAI with Google Cloud Integration
**What happened —** Genesys is updating its CCAI platform with new wrap-up and chat routing tools, integrating with Google Cloud's offerings. **What it means for Kore.ai —** This integration highlights the reliance of incumbents on hyperscaler partnerships, whereas Kore.ai's provider-agnostic platform and native voice capabilities offer a more robust and flexible solution without vendor lock-in.

## Watch for
- Continued integration and messaging around ServiceNow's "Otto" platform and its impact on enterprise adoption.
- Salesforce's ongoing efforts to clarify and stabilize Agentforce pricing models.
- Genesys's ability to demonstrate seamless integration with Google Cloud beyond basic routing.
- Sierra's progress in building out its long-horizon agent platform and securing enterprise customer proof points.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's analyst leadership in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Forrester Wave Q2 2026) to counter the narrative of incumbent dominance.
- Highlight Kore.ai's deterministic ABL and compile-time validation as a key differentiator against the potential for "assist credit" overruns and agent drift seen in ServiceNow and Salesforce.
- Position Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities (voice and digital) against the continued reliance of players like Genesys on hyperscaler integrations for core functionalities.
- Arm sales with clear TCO comparisons that account for the unpredictable consumption models of Salesforce and ServiceNow, contrasting them with Kore.ai's predictable pricing.
