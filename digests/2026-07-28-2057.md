# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Tuesday, 28 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are increasingly highlighting their AI agent capabilities, but often bolt-on or with significant pricing and governance concerns. Kore.ai's integrated, governed, and analyst-validated platform remains the clear differentiator against these emerging threats.

### **What happened —** NICE announced the integration of its acquired Cognigy capabilities into its CXone platform, aiming to deliver a unified agentic AI experience. **What it means for Kore.ai —** This integration validates the market's move towards unified agentic AI platforms. Kore.ai's native, single-stack approach avoids the complexities and potential roadmap ambiguities of stitching together acquired technologies, offering a more robust and predictable solution.

### Agentforce Moves to Production, Highlighting Salesforce's Agentic Ambitions

### **What happened —** Salesforce's Agentforce platform has transitioned from pilot to production, with the company pledging significant investment in Switzerland and emphasizing the platform's enterprise readiness. This move signals Salesforce's aggressive push to embed agentic AI across its ecosystem, including contact centers [MarketScale](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi5gFBVV95cUxPWXRyN2J4UUZ2dEZTWVpqcENaQmRHOEdlRGdkSnlJUHh0Vzk0YXJjX2tlcGVYSkJSQ0lrbndKdFFMZXNnQ1JYbjBkQnRiZXp6YzVHMUd0WU9VMF93RHhOMkhXT3hHaHI4UlNpN1lLTEdBQk9xTGxjMWxPMnZJbEViangxMlRBalVIVzVNRzduTTNXdk5RWG1TOVVXNmRWMnNneVJYd2lCZ2xXUk0zRmFENzF1VFVocV9MMEhyeTJXM25HYmtUSTBpTHNNUFAzQXQ4d19nSmp1Y3I5djVVaTZrN3N5TWItUQ?oc=5).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** Salesforce's broad enterprise reach and CRM integration are a competitive factor. However, Kore.ai's strength lies in its platform-agnostic approach and superior analyst validation in the *conversational AI for CX* category, offering a more specialized and less lock-in-prone solution compared to Salesforce's ecosystem-dependent strategy.

### ServiceNow Highlights AI Governance as a Key Buying Trigger

### **What happened —** ServiceNow's Q2 earnings report indicated that AI governance is increasingly becoming a critical factor for enterprise customers in their purchasing decisions. This suggests a growing market awareness and demand for robust control and compliance features in AI deployments ERP Today.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This aligns perfectly with Kore.ai's core strengths in determinism, auditability, and comprehensive governance, particularly through its Agent Behavior Language (ABL). We should continue to emphasize our pre-production validation and built-in compliance features as a key differentiator against competitors whose governance is often an add-on or less mature.

## Watch for
- Continued integration progress and customer adoption of NICE's combined Cognigy/CXone offering.
- Salesforce's specific customer wins and use cases for Agentforce in the contact center space.
- Any further analyst commentary on the governance capabilities of ServiceNow's AI offerings.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **unified, native platform** versus the "bolted-on" approach of acquired technologies like NICE Cognigy.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **leading analyst validation** in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Forrester Wave, Gartner MQ) as proof of platform maturity and CX focus, contrasting with Salesforce's CRM-centric positioning.
- Double down on messaging around **built-in governance and determinism** as key differentiators, directly addressing the growing customer demand highlighted by ServiceNow.
- Arm sales with specific questions about competitors' **AI pricing models** and their potential for overages or unpredictability as automation scales.
