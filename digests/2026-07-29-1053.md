## Watch for
- Salesforce's continued integration of Agentforce into its broader CRM and contact center offerings.
- NICE's progress in integrating Cognigy's capabilities into its CXone platform.
- Any signs of broader adoption or specific use cases emerging from the Salesforce Agentforce Contact Center deployments.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's analyst-validated leadership in Conversational AI for Customer Service, particularly against competitors who lack this independent validation.
- Highlight the inherent risks and potential for cost overruns with consumption-based or credit-based AI pricing models, contrasting with Kore.ai's predictable TCO.
- Showcase Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities, particularly voice-native functionality, as a differentiator against voice-first or digitally-focused solutions.
- Reinforce the strength of Kore.ai's Agent Behavior Language (ABL) for deterministic execution and robust governance, especially for regulated industries.
