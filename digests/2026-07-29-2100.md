## Watch for
- Salesforce's continued integration of Agentforce into its broader CRM and contact center offerings.
- ServiceNow's progress in unifying its AI capabilities under the "Otto" umbrella and its impact on CX.
- Cresta's ability to demonstrate tangible ROI and enterprise-grade reliability beyond conversation intelligence.

## Recommended actions
- Arm Sales with messaging that highlights Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities and independent analyst leadership against platform-specific agentic solutions.
- Emphasize the deterministic and auditable nature of Kore.ai's ABL framework in all customer conversations, particularly when competitors tout LLM-driven capabilities.
- Continue to showcase customer success stories that demonstrate the breadth of Kore.ai's platform across voice and digital channels, countering competitors with narrower scopes.
- Reinforce the message that Kore.ai is the pure-play conversational AI platform leader for CX, as validated by Gartner and Forrester, distinct from broader enterprise AI platforms.
