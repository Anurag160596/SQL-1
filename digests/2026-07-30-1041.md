# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Thursday, 30 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing AI agent capabilities and expanding platform reach, but significant gaps remain in true omnichannel voice integration, predictable TCO, and independent analyst validation for CX-specific AI. Kore.ai's ABL and proven platform breadth continue to be key differentiators against these emerging and incumbent players.

**What happened —** Salesforce announced that BusinessCapital.com is accelerating its deal velocity using Agentforce Sales. [salesforce.com](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMibEFVX3lxTE95WVVJMzk4d1dRMVBESVhKY0d3WE4tdDlIQWt2dGJPQkdDUnNQbTZFMFZfRk1HU3ljVklteDFMdktweGc4YUxfckdSVmRHR3lSb24xLUljOVdybGxraFFwU2pSSldSTDNLUDlyWg?oc=5) (2026-07-29)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights Salesforce's continued push for agentic capabilities within their CRM, but it's focused on internal sales processes, not external customer-facing voice or true omnichannel CX. Kore.ai's platform advantage lies in its native voice capabilities and unified omnichannel approach, which Agentforce lacks, relying on third-party integrations.

## Watch for
- Continued integration and messaging around ServiceNow's acquisition of Moveworks.
- Further details on Salesforce's Agentforce roadmap and customer adoption for CX use cases.
- Cresta's continued emphasis on its "Insights" capabilities and how it translates to agentic actions.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **true omnichannel voice and digital capabilities** in all customer conversations, highlighting the limitations of voice-first or digital-only solutions.
- Reinforce the **predictability and transparency of Kore.ai's ABL-driven pricing and TCO** against competitors with consumption-based models and "assist" credits.
- Leverage **independent analyst validation** (Gartner, Forrester) as a key differentiator against competitors lacking CX-specific leadership placements.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **enterprise-grade governance and determinism** as critical for regulated industries, contrasting with the potential for "prompt + custom code" approaches to introduce risk.
