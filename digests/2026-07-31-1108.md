# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Friday, 31 July 2026)

> **Bottom line:** PolyAI's new audio-native dialog model, Dialog-RSN-1, directly challenges established transcription-based voice AI by focusing on real-time intonation and emotion, creating an opening for Kore.ai to highlight its own voice-native capabilities and robust omnichannel experience. Salesforce's Agentforce continues to show strong deal velocity, but its pricing model and reliance on Data Cloud present opportunities for Kore.ai to emphasize predictable TCO and platform flexibility. Cresta's new insights offering reinforces its strength in conversation intelligence, but Kore.ai can counter by underscoring its broader platform capabilities and deterministic agent behavior.

## Watch for
- Continued evolution of PolyAI's audio-native model into broader enterprise CX use cases.
- Salesforce's strategy to address Agentforce pricing predictability and reduce customer anxiety.
- Analyst evaluations of new voice AI models and their impact on market leadership.

## Recommended actions
- Arm Sales with messaging that highlights Kore.ai's *true* omnichannel voice and digital capabilities, directly countering PolyAI's voice-first approach.
- Develop TCO comparison tools that clearly illustrate the predictable cost structure of Kore.ai's platform against the potential overages and complexities of Agentforce's consumption-based pricing.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's platform breadth and deterministic agent behavior in competitive narratives against Cresta's strong but focused conversation intelligence offering.
- Continue to emphasize Kore.ai's leadership in independent analyst evaluations for Conversational AI Platforms, particularly Forrester's Q2 2026 Wave and Gartner's 2025 MQ.
