# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 01 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing distinct architectural approaches to voice AI and agent development, creating opportunities for Kore.ai to highlight its unified, enterprise-grade platform and compile-time validation against emerging complexities and cost unpredictability.

### PolyAI's Audio-Native Architecture
**What happened —** (2026-07-31) PolyAI launched Dialog-RSN-1, an audio-native dialog model that bypasses transcription, directly processing speech to reduce latency and better capture intonation and emotion for more human-like AI calls SiliconANGLE.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights a trend towards voice-first, lower-latency AI, an area where Kore.ai's voice-native capabilities and true omnichannel approach can be positioned as superior to voice-only solutions that lack digital integration.

### Agentforce's Revenue Growth Amidst Platform Evolution
**What happened —** (2026-07-30) Salesforce reported that Agentforce closed 8,000 deals last quarter, contributing to $1.2B in ARR, while the stock remains down 26% year-to-date, and Moody’s sales representatives are booking 20% more meetings using Agentforce TIKR.com Salesforce.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** Agentforce's significant ARR growth, despite stock performance and pricing model shifts, underscores the market demand for agentic AI. Kore.ai can leverage this by emphasizing its stable, transparent pricing and proven analyst validation in conversational AI for CX, contrasting with Agentforce's evolving and potentially complex pricing structures.

### IBM's Strategic Focus on Enterprise AI Governance
**What happened —** (2026-07-31) IBM's enterprise AI strategy is prioritizing security, data control, and trust, as highlighted by their Docling integration for watsonx on AWS, which prepares enterprise documents for AI processing finance.yahoo.com Amazon Web Services (AWS).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** IBM's emphasis on security and data control aligns with Kore.ai's core strengths in governance and determinism via ABL. This presents an opportunity to highlight Kore.ai's compile-time validation and auditable AI behaviors as superior to solutions that rely on post-hoc data preparation and may still face inherent LLM uncertainties.

## Watch for
- Continued evolution of voice-native architectures and their integration into broader CX platforms.
- Salesforce's ability to stabilize Agentforce's pricing and demonstrate consistent ROI beyond initial deal closures.
- IBM's progress in integrating its agentic control plane with contact-center specific capabilities.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's unified, voice-and-digital omnichannel platform in all messaging, contrasting with voice-first or siloed solutions.
- Highlight ABL's compile-time validation and deterministic execution as key differentiators for enterprise-grade governance and cost predictability, especially against emerging LLM uncertainties.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in independent CX conversational AI evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) to counter competitors with strong funding but less analyst validation in the core CX space.
- Arm sales teams with talking points on the TCO unpredictability of consumption-based AI models and the risks of evolving pricing structures from large platform vendors.
