# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 01 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** PolyAI's new audio-native model highlights a growing trend toward more human-like, low-latency voice AI, directly challenging Kore.ai's voice-native capabilities and underscoring the need to emphasize our platform's comprehensive omnichannel and deterministic governance advantages. ServiceNow's AI revenue milestone, while impressive, reinforces its ITSM-centric approach and the inherent limitations for true CX automation, presenting an opportunity to highlight our CX-native, model-agnostic platform.

## PolyAI's Audio-Native Model Challenges Voice AI Norms
### PolyAI's Audio-Native Model Challenges Voice AI Norms
**What happened —** PolyAI released Dialog-RSN-1, an audio-native dialog model that bypasses transcription, directly processing audio to reflect intonation and emotions, aiming for more human-like AI-driven calls with reduced latency. [SiliconANGLE](2026-07-30)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This move directly targets our voice-native strengths; we must aggressively highlight our sub-500ms scripted turns and true omnichannel session resume to differentiate from voice-first approaches and emphasize our platform's broader CX capabilities beyond just voice.

### ServiceNow Hits $1B AI Revenue Milestone
**What happened —** ServiceNow announced its AI revenue has surpassed the $1 billion mark, driven by its AI offerings integrated across its platform. [AD HOC NEWS](2026-08-01)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While a significant revenue achievement, this reinforces ServiceNow's ITSM-centric AI strategy. We can position this as evidence that their AI is primarily focused on internal workflows, not purpose-built for complex, external customer-facing omnichannel conversations, where Kore.ai's platform excels.

### Decagon Leverages Open Source for Enterprise AI
**What happened —** Decagon reports running 90% of its enterprise AI on open-source technologies, emphasizing flexibility and cost-effectiveness. [startuphub.ai](2026-07-31)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights a potential cost advantage for Decagon, but also a potential governance and reliability gap. We can counter by emphasizing Kore.ai's enterprise-grade, secure, and governed platform, which offers deterministic execution and robust compliance features that open-source solutions may struggle to provide at scale for regulated industries.
