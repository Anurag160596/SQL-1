# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 03 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing their agentic AI narratives, with ServiceNow and Salesforce highlighting platform integration and broad enterprise reach. However, their reliance on consumption-based pricing and inherent platform lock-in presents a significant opportunity for Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and provider-agnostic approach.

## Three signals that matter

### ServiceNow Unifies Agentic AI Under "Otto"
**What happened —** ServiceNow announced the unification of its AI offerings, including the recent acquisition of Moveworks, under the new "Otto" brand, aiming to deliver a single, integrated agentic AI experience across its platform for both employee and customer-facing use cases. This move consolidates their AI strategy and emphasizes platform integration as a key differentiator. ### Salesforce Agentforce Drives $1B ARR with Pricing Flexibility
**What happened —** Salesforce reported that Agentforce has achieved $1.2 billion in ARR, with a significant portion driven by flexible pricing models that include consumption-based "Flex Credits" and per-user SKUs, indicating strong adoption despite past pricing model changes. ### ServiceNow Expands European Reach, Bolstering AI Narrative
**What happened —** ServiceNow is intensifying its European distribution efforts, aiming to strengthen its market presence and capitalize on its AI-driven growth narrative across the continent. ## Watch for
- Continued integration of Moveworks into ServiceNow's "Otto" platform and its impact on their CX narrative.
- Salesforce's ongoing efforts to clarify and stabilize Agentforce pricing models amidst customer feedback.
- Analyst commentary on the effectiveness of platform consolidation strategies in the enterprise AI space.

## Recommended actions
- Arm Sales with messaging that highlights Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and TCO advantages over consumption-based models like Salesforce's Flex Credits.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's position as a pure-play, CX-native platform with true omnichannel capabilities, contrasting this with ServiceNow's ITSM-centric origins and reliance on third-party voice.
- Continue to emphasize Kore.ai's strong analyst validation in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Gartner, Forrester) as a key differentiator against platform players with broader, but less specialized, AI offerings.
- Develop talking points that address the potential complexity and hidden costs associated with large platform AI integrations, positioning Kore.ai as a more focused and transparent solution for CX automation.
