# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Tuesday, 04 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, with Sierra securing significant funding and ServiceNow integrating Moveworks. This reinforces the need for Kore.ai to emphasize its proven analyst leadership in conversational AI for CX and its deterministic, cost-predictable platform against emerging hype and complex integrations.

## Three signals that matter

**What it means for Kore.ai —** ServiceNow is solidifying its agentic AI strategy, folding in Moveworks to bolster its ITSM-centric offerings. Our counter is to emphasize Kore.ai's purpose-built, CX-native agentic platform with true omnichannel voice and digital capabilities, contrasting with ServiceNow's ITSM roots and reliance on third-party voice solutions. We should highlight our superior analyst validation in Conversational AI for Customer Service.

## Watch for
*   Continued expansion of Sierra's financial services capabilities and any early customer proof points.
*   ServiceNow's progress in integrating Moveworks and its impact on their agentic AI narrative for CX.
*   Any further analyst commentary on the "chaos" of AI agent pricing, as highlighted by MarketWatch.

## Recommended actions
*   Arm Sales with specific talking points contrasting Kore.ai's deterministic ABL and governance with the potential risks of LLM-native agents in regulated industries like finance.
*   Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in Conversational AI for Customer Service by leveraging recent analyst reports (Gartner MQ, Forrester Wave) in all external communications, especially against platform players like ServiceNow and IBM.
*   Develop messaging that highlights Kore.ai's purpose-built, true omnichannel (voice and digital) CX platform, contrasting it with ITSM-centric or voice-first solutions.
*   Prepare competitive battlecards that directly address the pricing unpredictability and potential TCO challenges of consumption-based AI models, as indicated by the MarketWatch article.
