# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Tuesday, 04 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Sierra's new partnership with Plaid enables its AI agents to perform direct financial transactions, signaling a significant push into actionable, regulated workflows, while ServiceNow faces internal scrutiny over its AI Control Tower's effectiveness, highlighting potential TCO unpredictability for customers.

## Three signals that matter

### Sierra's Agents Gain Direct Financial Transaction Capabilities
**What happened —** (2026-08-04) Sierra has partnered with Plaid, allowing its AI agents to connect directly to bank accounts and execute financial workflows mid-conversation, as reported by FF News and [AIM Media House](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisAFBVV95cUxOYVVMbkl4NTBtWk1wT3JFb0FWQXZxcVdlTGZQNmJwWHpCQUJjWS1yQjBJVFdMRzVPU3Q0WHA4VnJVVUxWOWg0RkNsa0l1N3ZtQUdjS3I5UVpzQXhrVXV3dGYzNnZfTTNwM240eU5YNnFPSlBlLXJmRTZZcFdLREZaX2ZwZGZQX3hWYlhKUGI2QWZQWTM3bWVlMnI1c0NqRV9MWnVzM3ByNlNTMHlrYl9JbQ?oc=5). This enables agents to handle tasks like checking balances or initiating payments.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This move strengthens Sierra's transactional capabilities in regulated finance, directly challenging our strengths in deterministic, auditable workflows. We must emphasize Kore.ai's ABL compile-time validation and zero-LLM deterministic mode for compliance-critical paths.

### ServiceNow's AI Control Tower Faces Internal Effectiveness Questions
**What happened —** (2026-08-04) ServiceNow's AI Control Tower, designed to manage AI spend and ROI, is reportedly struggling with its own internal payroll deployment, according to AD HOC NEWS.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This provides a strong undercut opportunity against ServiceNow's "assists" consumption model and platform claims, highlighting potential TCO unpredictability and operational challenges even for internal use cases. Kore.ai offers transparent, per-agent cost attribution.
