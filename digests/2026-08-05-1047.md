# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Wednesday, 05 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Sierra's significant funding and new Plaid integration highlight a growing trend of CX-native AI agents gaining access to sensitive data, creating an opening for Kore.ai to emphasize its robust governance and deterministic execution for regulated industries. Meanwhile, Five9's continued struggles with its seat-based model and organizational instability present an opportunity to highlight Kore.ai's outcome-based pricing and platform stability.

## Three signals that matter

**What it means for Kore.ai —** Five9's pursuit of a noninfringement ruling, coupled with its seat-based model's vulnerability to AI, reinforces Kore.ai's position as a stable, outcome-focused platform. Kore.ai can emphasize its predictable, per-resolution pricing and its robust, non-litigious approach to AI development, highlighting the risks associated with competitors facing potential IP disputes and legacy pricing models.
