# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Wednesday, 05 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, particularly in regulated sectors and voice, but often struggle with pricing predictability and independent validation, creating clear openings for Kore.ai's proven, deterministic platform.
