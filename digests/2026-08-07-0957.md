- Plaid and Sierra's continued integration of financial workflows into their AI agents.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's proven voice-native capabilities and end-to-end omnichannel experience in all sales and marketing collateral.
- Highlight Kore.ai's leadership positions in Gartner and Forrester evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service to counter competitors' market claims.
- Reinforce messaging around ABL's compile-time validation and deterministic execution for regulated industries, contrasting with the potential for LLM drift and unpredictable costs from competitors.
- Leverage the ongoing integration challenges and adoption pace of acquired technologies (e.g., NICE/Cognigy) as a point of differentiation for Kore.ai's unified platform.
- Continue to monitor Sierra's enterprise customer wins and their impact on market perception, ensuring our value proposition addresses their specific strengths.
