# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Friday, 07 August 2026)

Kore.ai's strength lies in its proven, analyst-validated platform breadth and deterministic ABL, offering a stark contrast to the evolving, often complex, and sometimes unproven offerings from these giants.

## Watch for
- Continued push by Salesforce on Agentforce's government and regulated industry traction.
- ServiceNow's integration progress of Moveworks and its impact on enterprise adoption beyond ITSM.
- NICE's ability to effectively merge Cognigy's capabilities into its existing CXone platform without creating customer confusion.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven, analyst-validated leadership in Conversational AI for Customer Service** (Gartner MQ, Forrester Wave) as a counter to platform giants with broader but less specialized AI offerings.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **deterministic ABL and compile-time validation** as critical for regulated industries and enterprises concerned with AI governance, directly addressing the security claims of competitors like Salesforce.
- Position Kore.ai as the **true omnichannel leader**, contrasting our integrated voice and digital capabilities with the ITSM-centric or voice-first approaches of ServiceNow and Sierra, respectively.
- Arm sales with specific questions around **TCO predictability and AI model governance** for competitors employing consumption-based or less transparent pricing models.
