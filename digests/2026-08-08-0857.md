# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 08 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors like Five9 and NICE are highlighting AI revenue growth and new agent capabilities, but their underlying business models and integration strategies present clear opportunities for Kore.ai's proven, unified platform and deterministic ABL.

### Five9's AI Revenue Surge Masks Seat-Model Vulnerability
**What happened —** (2026-08-07) Five9 reported Q2 guidance beat with AI revenue jumping 78%, and secured a new $100 million contract driven by voice AI agents, as detailed by CMSWire.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights the market's demand for AI in CX, but Five9's continued reliance on a seat-based model, even with AI add-ons, is a vulnerability. Kore.ai's outcome-based pricing and ABL's deterministic execution offer superior TCO and reliability, especially as AI drives higher containment.

### NICE Bolsters AI Credibility with Cognigy Integration and Enterprise Deals
**What happened —** (2026-08-06) NICE reported strong Q2 revenue growth and raised its EPS outlook, driven by cloud and AI, while also securing significant CX AI deals. The Globe and Mail also noted that NICE landed its biggest deal ever, though Wall Street seeks more proof of enterprise adoption. CMSWire and CX Today covered these developments.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** NICE's acquisition of Cognigy is a strategic move to bolster its AI narrative. However, the integration of two AI lineages (Cognigy and Enlighten) presents potential roadmap ambiguity and governance challenges that Kore.ai's single-stack, ABL-driven platform inherently avoids, offering clearer path to production-ready, auditable AI.

### Sierra Lands CarMax, Signaling Continued Enterprise Land Grab in Conversational AI
**What happened —** (2026-08-06) Sierra has partnered with CarMax to enhance its inbound call experience using AI voice agents, as reported by Quiver Quantitative and CMSWire.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This win underscores the intense competition for enterprise logos in conversational AI. Kore.ai's established analyst leadership in Conversational AI Platforms, coupled with its proven omnichannel capabilities and deterministic ABL, provides a more robust and validated solution for enterprises seeking true end-to-end CX automation beyond voice-first or nascent agent platforms.

- Emphasize Kore.ai's ABL for deterministic, auditable AI and predictable TCO in sales conversations, contrasting with the potential overages and integration complexities of competitors like Five9 and NICE.
