# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 08 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI capabilities, with Five9 reporting strong AI revenue growth and Genesys highlighting AI ARR, but both remain tethered to legacy models or infrastructure risks. Salesforce's Agentforce continues its rapid iteration with new features and a complex pricing evolution, while Sierra lands a major enterprise client, underscoring the continued momentum in agentic CX platforms.

## Three signals that matter

### Five9 AI Revenue Jumps 78%, Highlighting Growth in Agentic CX Push
**What happened —** Five9 reported a 78% year-over-year increase in AI revenue for Q2 2026, contributing to overall revenue growth and beating guidance. This signals continued adoption of their AI capabilities as they pivot towards "Agentic CX." [Five9 Beats Q2 Guidance as AI Revenue Jumps 78%](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxQS3Z0Y0ZhS2RGUHBTOVBzbzItN21vRGg4Wi1UeUpPaGN0a1l0RTJmTXVBWXVRamtsOG5CeU11bE42T2lTX3d2YUY0ZVEtbk5aRHFCdzBKMUZwR2t2U2lmb1ZBdWJJd1RmZV9uUnNlV040SDhQTWJUZTBQVE5IR2FabDRtSElEejdweks4ZVhSbXJSb3ZyMVVmTnE1WWVhc1lhOFg0bDZuN2NBMzQ?oc=5) (2026-08-07)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While Five9's AI revenue is growing, their core seat-based model remains a structural vulnerability. Kore.ai's outcome-based and per-task pricing, coupled with our ABL's deterministic execution, offers superior TCO predictability and control for enterprises scaling AI deflection.

### Genesys Agents Empowered by AI Amidst Loyalty Program Focus
**What happened —** Genesys asserts that empowered agents, rather than loyalty programs, are key to customer retention, implying a strategic shift towards agent augmentation and AI capabilities within their platform. Customer Loyalty Programs Won't Save You. Empowered Agents Will. (2026-08-07)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** Genesys's emphasis on "empowered agents" aligns with our platform's core strength in providing robust agent-assist and AI agent capabilities. Kore.ai's ABL offers a more integrated and deterministic approach to agent empowerment, ensuring compliance and efficiency, unlike bolt-on solutions.

### Sierra Lands CarMax for Inbound Call Experience Enhancement
**What happened —** Sierra has partnered with CarMax to improve their inbound call experience using AI voice agents, signaling a significant enterprise adoption for Sierra's conversational AI platform. [CarMax Partners with Sierra to Improve Inbound Call Experience with AI Voice Agents](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitwFBVV95cUxNbVdaX1hEX25nSzJNdllhRV9yaEptQU1uU1hRU1U1a2JmQTB3T0VBblQ2NS1OeXNYSThETmJvZnVXTFJjc0k1ZXdnUHRwV0ZHclFnNGozQlRlRUJLSlkzNVdwMVJZRm14ZjZWRGxrSmcyTXlhT29YbFNQVWxIMDRka2JkTnYzSVNyTVVndnBaeFVPNUVvUUVFTzh5SzlKRlRVVGNJYjdfbHBDdlZ5Vjc1Qy16RmZPa00?oc=5) (2026-08-06)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This win for Sierra highlights the growing enterprise demand for voice-native AI agents. Kore.ai's established voice-native capabilities and proven track record with global enterprises provide a strong counter-positioning against newer entrants, emphasizing our platform's maturity and scalability.
