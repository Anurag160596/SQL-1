# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 09 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing AI capabilities, but often as add-ons to existing platforms or with pricing models that create customer anxiety. Kore.ai's integrated, purpose-built agentic platform with transparent, outcome-aligned pricing remains a key differentiator against these fragmented and potentially costly approaches.

### Five9 AI Revenue Surges, Highlighting Seat-Model Vulnerability
**What happened —** (2026-08-07) Five9 reported a 78% jump in AI revenue, beating Q2 guidance, as it continues to pivot towards "Agentic CX." CMSWire
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This rapid AI revenue growth for Five9, a CCaaS incumbent, underscores the market's demand for AI capabilities. However, their continued reliance on a seat-based model, even with AI add-ons, creates an exploitable weakness around TCO predictability as AI deflection increases. Kore.ai's ABL and outcome-aligned pricing offer a more stable and transparent economic model.

### Sierra Lands CarMax, Signaling Broadening Enterprise Land Grab in Conversational AI
**What happened —** (2026-08-06) Sierra has secured CarMax as a customer, indicating a widening enterprise adoption trend for conversational AI platforms. CMSWire
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This win for Sierra, a well-funded startup, demonstrates the market's appetite for advanced conversational AI. Kore.ai can counter by emphasizing its proven enterprise-grade platform, extensive analyst validation as a Leader in Conversational AI for Customer Service, and its ability to deliver deterministic, governed AI solutions, particularly for regulated industries where Sierra's autonomous LLM agents may raise concerns.

## Watch for
- Continued expansion of Salesforce Agentforce into new geographies and specific voice use cases.
- Any indication of pricing model simplification or clarification from Salesforce Agentforce.
- Further enterprise customer announcements from Sierra, particularly in regulated industries.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's robust, purpose-built agentic platform and its superior TCO predictability compared to seat-based models and complex consumption credits.
- Highlight Kore.ai's leadership positions in independent analyst evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service as proof of platform maturity and breadth.
- Reinforce the message of Kore.ai's provider-agnosticism and its ability to integrate with best-of-breed models, contrasting with platform lock-in strategies.
- Arm sales teams with specific questions to probe customer concerns around AI cost management and platform integration complexity with Salesforce and other incumbents.
