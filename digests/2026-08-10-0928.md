## Watch for
- Continued geographic expansion by Salesforce (Agentforce Voice in Japan).
- Further funding rounds for emerging agentic AI startups like Sierra and Decagon, and the continued gap in their CX analyst validation.
- ServiceNow's integration of Moveworks and its impact on their enterprise AI strategy.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's leadership in independent Conversational AI for CX analyst evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) as a key differentiator against platform players and emerging startups.
- Highlight Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities and voice-native architecture to counter voice-first or voice-limited competitors.
- Arm Sales with clear TCO comparisons, focusing on the predictability of Kore.ai's per-outcome pricing versus the overage risks of consumption-based models from ServiceNow and Salesforce.
- Reinforce the message of ABL's compile-time validation and deterministic execution for regulated industries, contrasting with the probabilistic nature of LLM-centric approaches.
