# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 10 August 2026)

### Five9 AI Revenue Surges, Highlighting Seat-Model Vulnerability
**What happened —** (2026-08-07) Five9 reported a 78% jump in AI revenue, beating Q2 guidance, signaling strong adoption of their AI offerings. CMSWire
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This growth validates the market's appetite for AI in contact centers.

**What happened —** (2026-08-07) A Parloa study indicates that older consumers trust AI customer service the most, suggesting a broader acceptance of AI across demographics. MarTech Cube
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This finding supports Kore.ai's strategy of broad AI adoption.

**What happened —** (2026-08-09) Salesforce has launched Agentforce Voice in Japan, integrating Kotoba technology to enhance its conversational AI offerings in the region. International Business Times
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While Salesforce expands its voice capabilities regionally, Kore.ai's global, provider-agnostic platform and true omnichannel approach offer a more robust and scalable solution for enterprises worldwide, without regional technology dependencies.
