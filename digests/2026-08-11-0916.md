## Watch for
- Continued integration and adoption of Salesforce Agentforce across various enterprise functions.
- Further market penetration by Five9 in the financial services sector and their AI strategy evolution.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's proven track record and analyst-backed leadership in enterprise conversational AI for CX, particularly against platform players like Salesforce Agentforce that are still proving CX readiness.
- Highlight Kore.ai's superior Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and predictable pricing models, contrasting with the potential cost overruns of seat-based and consumption-heavy AI models.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's voice-native capabilities and global deployment expertise as key differentiators against emerging voice solutions.
- Arm sales teams with specific talking points on the limitations of "platform" AI solutions compared to Kore.ai's purpose-built, agentic AI platform for CX.
