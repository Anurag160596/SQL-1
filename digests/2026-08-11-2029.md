# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Tuesday, 11 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, but gaps in enterprise-grade governance, true omnichannel, and predictable TCO persist, creating openings for Kore.ai's validated, full-stack platform. Salesforce's Agentforce continues its pricing recalibration, while Five9 faces ongoing pressure on its seat-based model.

## Three signals that matter

### Salesforce Agentforce Refines Pricing Again
**What happened —** Salesforce is reportedly making further adjustments to its Agentforce pricing, moving towards more predictable per-user SKUs after previous iterations involving conversation credits and flex credits, aiming to address buyer concerns about consumption overruns. CX Today (2026-08-10)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This signals continued buyer hesitancy and complexity around Salesforce's consumption-based AI pricing, reinforcing Kore.ai's advantage with clear, per-agent/intent cost attribution and deterministic pricing models.

### Five9 Secures Large CCaaS Contract Amidst AI Transition
**What happened —** Five9 announced a significant $100M financial services CCaaS contract win, highlighting their continued strength in the traditional CCaaS market even as they navigate the shift towards agentic AI. CX Today (2026-08-10)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This win underscores Five9's existing CCaaS customer base but also emphasizes their reliance on traditional models, creating an opening for Kore.ai to highlight the inherent limitations of seat-based architectures in an AI-driven deflection future and our superior AI-native, outcome-based value.

### Cresta Partners for AI-Powered CX Transformation
**What happened —** Cresta has partnered with Firstsource to accelerate AI-powered customer experience transformation, focusing on leveraging AI for enhanced customer interactions and operational efficiency. PR Newswire (2026-08-11)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This partnership highlights Cresta's continued focus on agent-assist and conversational intelligence. Kore.ai can leverage its broader platform capabilities, compile-time validation, and true omnichannel support as differentiators against Cresta's more specialized, conversation-data-centric approach.

- Emphasize Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and per-agent cost attribution in all sales discussions, directly contrasting with Salesforce's ongoing pricing complexity.
