# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Friday, 14 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing integrations and expanding agentic capabilities. Kore.ai's ABL and analyst validation remain key differentiators against these evolving threats.

## Three signals that matter
### Regal integrates voice AI with Five9, highlighting CCaaS vendor's AI expansion
**What happened —** Regal has integrated its voice AI agents with Five9's contact center platform, aiming to bring AI voice automation to enterprise contact centers (SiliconANGLE).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This move underscores the trend of CCaaS vendors bolstering their AI capabilities through partnerships. Kore.ai's integrated, voice-native platform and proven omnichannel strength offer a more cohesive and reliable solution than bolted-on integrations.

### Cresta and Firstsource partner to bridge enterprise CX deployment gap
**What happened —** Cresta is partnering with Firstsource to combine Cresta's AI platform with Firstsource's operational infrastructure, intending to accelerate enterprise CX deployments (MarketScale).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This partnership highlights Cresta's continued focus on deployment acceleration, a known challenge for AI solutions. Kore.ai's ABL and compile-time validation offer inherent advantages in reducing deployment friction and ensuring production readiness, a key differentiator.

### IBM integrates Dun & Bradstreet data into watsonx Orchestrate
**What happened —** The Dun & Bradstreet Commercial Graph is now available in the watsonx Orchestrate Agent Catalog via MCP server, enhancing data integration for IBM's agentic platform (IBM).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** IBM continues to expand its agentic platform's data access, a common theme across vendors. Kore.ai's robust A2A federation and broad integration capabilities provide a more flexible and comprehensive approach to data access without vendor-specific data graph dependencies.

## Watch for
- Continued integration efforts by CCaaS vendors (Five9, NICE) with third-party AI agents.
- Sierra's expansion into new verticals and its ability to translate funding into broad enterprise adoption beyond niche applications.
- IBM's progress in unifying watsonx Orchestrate and Assistant, and its traction in the contact center space.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **voice-native omnichannel capabilities** and integrated platform architecture in all sales and marketing collateral, directly contrasting with bolted-on partner solutions.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **ABL and compile-time validation** as a key differentiator for reducing deployment friction and ensuring production-ready AI agents, especially against competitors focusing on deployment acceleration.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's **leading analyst positions** in conversational AI for customer service to counter competitors' narrative of broad enterprise adoption without independent validation.
- Continue to monitor and articulate the **cost-per-outcome advantages** of Kore.ai's platform versus consumption-based or seat-centric models that can lead to unpredictable TCO.
