# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 15 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are doubling down on AI agent capabilities, with IBM and OpenAI forming a strategic alliance to deepen AI integration into enterprises, and Five9 continuing its push into voice AI agents. Kore.ai's strength lies in its established analyst leadership and its robust, deterministic platform that offers clear TCO advantages over consumption-based or seat-model approaches.

## Watch for
- Continued integration of third-party AI models into enterprise platforms.
- Evolving pricing models for AI agent usage and their impact on TCO.
- Further consolidation or strategic partnerships in the agentic AI space.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven leadership in CX-specific conversational AI evaluations** (Gartner, Forrester) as a key differentiator against newer or less validated players.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **deterministic Agent Behavior Language (ABL)** and compile-time validation as a critical advantage for regulated industries and predictable ROI, contrasting with consumption-based or probabilistic models.
- Position Kore.ai as the **provider-agnostic platform of choice**, offering flexibility and avoiding vendor lock-in, especially in light of strategic alliances like IBM and OpenAI.
- Arm sales teams with clear TCO comparisons that account for potential overages and unpredictable costs associated with consumption-based AI pricing models.
