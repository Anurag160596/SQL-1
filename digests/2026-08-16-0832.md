## Watch for
- Continued consolidation and partnerships among CCaaS providers and AI specialists to bolster CX capabilities.
- Evolving pricing models as vendors grapple with the cost and value of generative AI in enterprise deployments.
- Increased focus on governance and determinism as regulated industries adopt AI agents.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's analyst-validated leadership in conversational AI for CX, highlighting our comprehensive, omnichannel platform.
- Reinforce the value of ABL for compile-time validation and deterministic execution, positioning it as a critical differentiator against prompt-based solutions.
- Arm sales with clear messaging on the TCO predictability and governance advantages of Kore.ai's unified platform versus fragmented or bolt-on AI agent solutions.
- Continue to monitor the strategic alliances of major tech players and their implications for the enterprise AI landscape, particularly in CX.
