# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 16 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors like Five9 are pushing AI voice capabilities, but their underlying business models and product maturity present clear openings for Kore.ai's proven, omnichannel, and deterministic platform. IBM's strategic partnership with OpenAI signals a broader trend of leveraging large language models for enterprise AI, an area where Kore.ai's ABL provides a distinct advantage in control and predictability. Decagon's continued focus on open-source models highlights a segment of the market prioritizing flexibility, which can be contrasted with Kore.ai's enterprise-grade governance and reliability.

## Watch for
- Continued integration of voice AI by CCaaS players, and how they address cost and determinism.
- Further partnerships between large tech companies and LLM providers, and how they position their enterprise AI offerings.
- Decagon's ability to scale its open-source model strategy into enterprise-grade governance and compliance.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's ABL as the key differentiator for controlled, deterministic, and auditable AI agent behavior, especially when competitors leverage open-ended LLMs.
- Highlight Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities and cost-predictability against the backdrop of seat-based models with consumption-based AI add-ons.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in analyst evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service, contrasting with competitors who may lead in adjacent categories but lack specific CX validation.
- Arm sales teams with talking points that contrast the "AI bolt-on" approach of CCaaS incumbents with Kore.ai's integrated, AI-native platform.
