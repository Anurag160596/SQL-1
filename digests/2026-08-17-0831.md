# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 17 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing AI agent capabilities, but significant gaps remain in demonstrable ROI, true omnichannel integration, and independent validation, creating clear openings for Kore.ai's proven platform.

- Continued integration of Cognigy into NICE CXone, and potential roadmap clarity or confusion arising from the dual AI lineages.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify messaging on predictable TCO and ROI:** Highlight Kore.ai's transparent pricing and deterministic cost models, directly contrasting with the overage risks associated with consumption-based AI token models seen in competitors like Genesys.
- **Reinforce governance and auditability:** Emphasize Kore.ai's ABL framework for built-in compliance and deterministic execution, positioning it as superior to bolt-on solutions or those relying on open-source models without robust enterprise guardrails.
- **Leverage analyst validation:** Continue to prominently feature Kore.ai's Leader placements in Gartner and Forrester evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service, contrasting this with competitors who are not recognized in these specific CX-focused categories.
- **Arm Sales with pricing comparison talking points:** Equip the sales team with clear data points that illustrate the potential for hidden costs and overages with competitors' token-based or seat-plus-AI-add-on models.
- **Showcase true omnichannel capabilities:** Differentiate Kore.ai's unified voice and digital platform against voice-first or voice-limited solutions, emphasizing seamless customer journeys across all channels.
