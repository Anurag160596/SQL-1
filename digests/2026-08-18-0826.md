- Continued integration of third-party data intelligence into platforms like Agentforce.
- Further reports on AI agent adoption rates from major platform vendors.
- Evolving strategies around AI agent autonomy versus controlled deployments.

- Emphasize Kore.ai's ABL for deterministic and governed AI agent behavior in sales and marketing.
- Highlight Kore.ai's leadership in independent analyst evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service.
