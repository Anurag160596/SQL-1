## Watch for
- Continued market pressure on traditional seat-license revenue models from AI agent adoption.
- ServiceNow's specific AI strategy and execution in the APAC region.
- PolyAI's ability to expand its healthcare integrations and move beyond voice-first.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and its advantage over seat-license models vulnerable to AI deflection.
- Highlight Kore.ai's unified, omnichannel platform as a key differentiator against voice-first or niche solutions.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in enterprise CX conversational AI through analyst validation in all customer-facing materials.
- Position ABL as the solution for deterministic, auditable AI agent behavior, countering concerns about AI reliability in regulated industries.
