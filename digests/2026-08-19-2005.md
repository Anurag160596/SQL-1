# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Wednesday, 19 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Salesforce's aggressive rebranding of its agentic platform to "AgentForce" signals a strategic push to consolidate its AI narrative and capture enterprise CX mindshare. This move, coupled with ServiceNow's continued integration of AI agents into its core platform, underscores the growing importance of agentic AI as a foundational enterprise technology, creating an opportunity for Kore.ai to highlight its pure-play platform's analyst-validated leadership and vendor-agnostic flexibility against these increasingly integrated, but potentially more locked-in, offerings.

## Salesforce Rebrands to AgentForce, Pushing AI Deeper into Enterprise

### Salesforce Consolidates AI Narrative with AgentForce Rebrand
**What happened —** Salesforce has rebranded its agentic platform to "AgentForce," aiming to unify its AI offerings under a single, enterprise-focused banner that emphasizes headless 360 AI agents and deeper integration into enterprise work. This rebranding includes a focus on "AgentForce Voice" to elevate its contact center capabilities. [Salesforce](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMidEFVX3lxTE11eVlqNVN3SGVGQ25CdmgyWHFwakRzQUY3S0xmenhNVUlXREFXaDdoNkpEWER4QTR0TlVSazgyVlJ2T0NyeFl0ZElZRFhZd0ZBUnVLa3dOMWR0emlqcnl4ZTh6eXJiSmlRTW04ZDNyX3pQUTBD?oc=5) (2026-08-19)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This rebranding highlights Salesforce's intent to compete head-on in the agentic AI space, but their platform's inherent ties to the Salesforce ecosystem present an opportunity. Kore.ai can emphasize its vendor-agnostic, pure-play platform leadership, validated by top analysts, offering greater flexibility and avoiding the platform lock-in associated with AgentForce.

### ServiceNow Bolsters Agent Platform with AI Integration
**What happened —** ServiceNow is deepening the integration of AI agents into its core platform, with recent news focusing on how AI deals challenge its traditional pricing models and the broader threat AI agents pose to existing seat-license revenue. This push includes advancements in their AI agent capabilities and platform integration. [Kalkine Media](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMingFBVV95cUxPV3pacXAxalA3ZlZ2eDloQnJuajczQWowZGNWc0tDRzBrM3pKbTNQV21tQ1J5WGNQeGxmaUtFZ201WUppUE5sVGFGQWJoazQzUXZoY3dFZmFKa1BsRmpIbkpUU1h5WUpsWWZPSzNDNTVBVTY3N3JISnA5Unh4T085RWwtZGtIbklhSkJWREcwSEpLNDZER0otMmlrUHRMQQ?oc=5) (2026-08-19)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** ServiceNow's focus on integrating AI agents into its ITSM-centric platform, while expanding into CX, presents an opening. Kore.ai can highlight its purpose-built, enterprise-grade conversational AI platform that offers superior depth in CX and contact center specific capabilities, alongside its proven ability to integrate with various ITSM and CRM systems, rather than forcing customers into a single platform's ecosystem.

### IBM Highlights Forward-Deployed Engineering for Client Transformation
**What happened —** IBM is emphasizing its "forward deployed engineering" approach as a key driver for redefining client transformation, suggesting a focus on deep technical integration and tailored solutions for enterprise clients leveraging their AI capabilities. [ibm.com](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxQVlJhLVVBSUNLUDM4TV9FWlA2dmU0aHpHX0tUaFpwaFJlWjBEcFBpT0Frcm90XzhUdnJ3dlNqTFFiWk9qeVQ3eUV2VWtPRjA1RFpqano2NW9IcnhfSFJlRmN6ampiYVZhVnBUbXFfaG5TVHFXN3lfVHJwdzJQUFl0MUFSNGt1bXQtaWdXTlBlS2N1SkxIX01DOEJEZGw1Xy01WmNlTGhQc2wwQVE?oc=5) (2026-08-19)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** IBM's focus on "forward deployed engineering" and client transformation, while valuable, can be framed as a reliance on consulting services rather than a self-service, scalable platform. Kore.ai can emphasize its platform's inherent ease of use, rapid development capabilities via ABL, and its proven ability to deliver enterprise-grade CX automation without the heavy reliance on bespoke engineering engagements, backed by strong analyst validation.

## Watch for
- Continued evolution of Salesforce's AgentForce pricing models and their impact on enterprise adoption.
- ServiceNow's progress in integrating Moveworks and its implications for the broader agentic AI market.
- Further analyst validation or shifts in positioning for enterprise agentic AI platforms.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify Messaging:** Double down on Kore.ai's analyst-validated leadership in Conversational AI Platforms (Gartner, Forrester) and its pure-play, vendor-agnostic platform strengths to counter the integrated offerings from Salesforce and ServiceNow.
- **Arm Sales:** Equip sales teams with clear talking points and battlecards that highlight the potential for platform lock-in and pricing unpredictability with Salesforce AgentForce and ServiceNow's AI agent offerings, contrasting them with Kore.ai's flexible, cost-predictable, and deeply integrated CX-native solution.
- **Highlight ABL Differentiation:** Continue to emphasize the compile-time validation and deterministic execution of Kore.ai's Agent Behavior Language (ABL) as a critical advantage for regulated industries and mission-critical CX applications, where Salesforce and ServiceNow may rely more heavily on probabilistic LLM outputs.
- **Showcase Omnichannel Depth:** Proactively demonstrate Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities, particularly its voice-native features and seamless digital channel integration, to differentiate from voice-first competitors like PolyAI and address any perceived gaps in the broader platform plays.
