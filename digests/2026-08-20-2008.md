# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Thursday, 20 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are doubling down on agentic AI integration and partnership plays, with ServiceNow and Tech Mahindra highlighting scaled enterprise AI delivery, while PolyAI faces challenges with accent recognition and Glean expands its partner network. Kore.ai's ABL and proven analyst leadership remain key differentiators against these evolving, but often fragmented, competitive efforts.

## Three signals that matter

### ServiceNow scales enterprise AI with Tech Mahindra
**What happened —** (2026-08-20) Tech Mahindra and ServiceNow announced an expanded partnership to deliver production-ready enterprise AI at scale, including the launch of a new AI & Innovation CoE. Tribal also launched ServiceNow AI Support. PR Newswire UK
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This signals a continued push by large platform vendors to leverage partner ecosystems for scaled AI deployments. Kore.ai's ABL provides a more integrated and governed approach to building and deploying AI agents, reducing the complexity and risk associated with partner-led integrations.

### PolyAI faces accent recognition hurdles
**What happened —** (2026-08-19) An AI GP receptionist powered by PolyAI reportedly struggled to understand a Yorkshire accent, highlighting potential limitations in voice AI's nuanced language processing. [AOL.ca](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMigwFBVV95cUxQWkFPaVRRTUg3NllhUVBwLXlPSlRmVy1xZGNkWjNldHpvZUZhalRLQWNMUHJjdXJCbFFIQi1SdF9Qa19EczlVTHk1YkhvS1JnTnhqTlpTMlhITmxta0tGS2dwWkZ2b3hyQ25FLTRTQ1VJUW5STENRc3FNbEdrQjFXWEg0aw?oc=5)
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This incident underscores the importance of robust, real-world voice processing capabilities. Kore.ai's voice-native platform and deep expertise in handling diverse speech patterns and accents provide a more reliable and inclusive customer experience, directly countering such limitations.

### Glean expands partner network for AI
**What happened —** (2026-08-19) Glean launched a Global Partner Network for its AI initiatives, signaling a strategic move to broaden its reach and integration capabilities within the enterprise AI space. Channel Insider
**What it means for Kore.ai —** While Glean focuses on enterprise search and internal agents, its partner expansion highlights the growing trend of ecosystem plays. Kore.ai's established, broad partner network and its ability to integrate across diverse enterprise systems and models offer a more mature and proven path to comprehensive CX automation.
