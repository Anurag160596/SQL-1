## Watch for
*   Continued analyst validation of Kore.ai and NiCE Cognigy as Leaders in Conversational AI for Customer Service, contrasting with the positioning of platform vendors like Salesforce and ServiceNow.
*   Any further evidence of pricing complexity or customer TCO challenges with Salesforce Agentforce's consumption-based model.
*   ServiceNow's progress in unifying its agentic AI offerings under the "Otto" umbrella and its impact on customer adoption and competitive differentiation.

## Recommended actions
*   Emphasize Kore.ai's **analyst leadership** in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Gartner, Forrester) as a key differentiator against platform vendors and niche players.
*   Reinforce messaging around **predictable TCO and ROI** for enterprise CX automation, directly contrasting with the potential for cost overruns in consumption-based models like Salesforce Agentforce.
*   Highlight Kore.ai's **true omnichannel capabilities** (voice and digital native) as a critical advantage over voice-first or primarily digital solutions.
*   Arm Sales with specific questions to probe competitors on their **voice strategy, pricing predictability, and independent CX analyst validation**.
*   Continue to monitor **ServiceNow's integration efforts** and position Kore.ai as the stable, unified, and purpose-built alternative for enterprise CX.
