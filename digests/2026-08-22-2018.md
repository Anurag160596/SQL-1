# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 22 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, but Salesforce's Agentforce faces continued partner revenue concerns, while ServiceNow's pricing model and IBM's product confusion present clear openings for Kore.ai's validated, pure-play CX AI platform.

## Three signals that matter

### Salesforce Agentforce Partner Revenue Concerns Persist
**What happened —** (2026-08-21) A report indicates that Salesforce partners are not seeing meaningful revenue from the Agentforce AI platform, suggesting adoption and monetization challenges persist. The Register
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights a persistent gap between Salesforce's platform ambition and tangible partner value, reinforcing Kore.ai's strength in delivering clear ROI and predictable revenue for its own ecosystem.

## Watch for
- Continued evolution and pricing adjustments for Salesforce Agentforce.
- ServiceNow's ability to translate partnerships into broad enterprise AI adoption beyond ITSM.
- Analyst validation for IBM's watsonx Orchestrate in the contact center space.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's proven ROI and predictable TCO in sales conversations, contrasting with Salesforce's partner revenue struggles and ServiceNow's consumption-based pricing risks.
- Highlight Kore.ai's unified, voice-native, and true omnichannel platform as a distinct advantage over IBM's reliance on third-party voice and product fragmentation.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in independent CX conversational AI evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) to counter competitors who lack this specific validation.
- Arm sales with specific questions that probe the true cost and revenue generation potential of competitor AI platforms for partners and customers.
