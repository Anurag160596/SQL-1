# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 23 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing agentic AI capabilities, but Salesforce's Agentforce faces significant headwinds with partner revenue and pricing complexity, while ServiceNow's integration of Moveworks signals a continued focus on internal enterprise workflows rather than external CX. PolyAI's strategic integration with Epic highlights a growing trend of vertical specialization, presenting an opportunity for Kore.ai's broad, platform-agnostic approach.

## Three signals that matter
### Salesforce Agentforce Partner Revenue Concerns
**What happened —** Salesforce partners are reportedly not seeing meaningful revenue from the Agentforce AI platform, according to a report from The Register on (2026-08-21).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This indicates potential friction in Salesforce's ecosystem and a lack of clear value realization for partners, underscoring Kore.ai's strength in providing a platform that drives tangible business outcomes and partner success through its robust integration capabilities and clear ROI.

**What it means for Kore.ai —** ServiceNow's focus on internal IT/HR workflows reinforces Kore.ai's differentiation as a comprehensive, purpose-built platform for external customer experience and contact center automation, particularly in voice and true omnichannel scenarios where ServiceNow has a stated reliance on third-party integrations.

### PolyAI Deepens Healthcare Integration with Epic
**What happened —** PolyAI launches direct integration with Epic, with PDS Health being one of the first to deploy at scale, as reported on (2026-08-21) [AOL.com](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiiwFBVV95cUxQWkFuMkNpeTVLOVRlRFZZMmhnV196ZjZIaV9MakRvb01NaXpmZkprNTJ3OFpxVWFTNXhQbXY2dS0wUzVaZVhkaVpNMWU2eVFaOFFRQ3RHbC1iRDAzNHRoRzFGVmtCVF9uM1NFWklDYnNfMTc2alFUUm5pLXlYM0tTUkFfSHVHTFE5ZlAw?oc=5).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This move highlights the growing trend of vertical-specific AI solutions. Kore.ai's platform-agnostic, comprehensive omnichannel approach, coupled with its pre-built vertical applications and strong analyst validation in conversational AI for CX, positions it as a superior alternative to niche, voice-first solutions that may lack the breadth and depth for broader enterprise CX needs.

## Watch for
- Salesforce's continued efforts to monetize Agentforce through its partner channel and refine its complex pricing models.
- ServiceNow's integration roadmap for Moveworks and its impact on their agentic AI strategy, particularly concerning external CX capabilities.
- Further expansion of voice-first AI solutions into specific industry verticals, challenging broader platform plays.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's proven ROI and partner enablement success in sales conversations, contrasting with reported challenges for Salesforce's Agentforce.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in true omnichannel customer experience and voice-native capabilities, highlighting ServiceNow's internal workflow focus.
- Position Kore.ai's platform-agnostic approach and broad vertical applicability as a strategic advantage against increasingly specialized, voice-first solutions.
- Highlight Kore.ai's analyst validation in Conversational AI for Customer Service as a key differentiator against platform vendors with narrower focus areas.
