# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 24 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Salesforce's Agentforce is showing strong ARR growth but faces persistent questions around pricing predictability and its reliance on third-party telephony for voice, creating an opening for Kore.ai's integrated, omnichannel, and transparently priced platform. ServiceNow's continued integration of its platform for enterprise AI adoption signals a deepening of its ITSM-centric approach, reinforcing Kore.ai's advantage in purpose-built, enterprise-wide CX AI.

## Watch for
- Continued evolution of Salesforce Agentforce pricing models and partner enablement.
- ServiceNow's integration roadmap and its impact on its CX capabilities.
- PolyAI's expansion beyond voice and into broader digital channels.

## Recommended actions
- Arm Sales with messaging that highlights Kore.ai's integrated, voice-native omnichannel platform and transparent, outcome-based pricing against Salesforce Agentforce's complexity and third-party dependencies.
- Emphasize Kore.ai's dedicated, enterprise-grade CX AI platform and proven success in transforming customer service operations when engaging with prospects evaluating ServiceNow.
- Leverage Kore.ai's broad omnichannel capabilities and pre-built healthcare vertical applications to counter PolyAI's voice-first, niche focus in healthcare deals.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's leadership in analyst evaluations for Conversational AI for Customer Service, particularly against competitors who are not Leaders in this specific category.
