# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Monday, 24 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing agentic AI capabilities, with Salesforce highlighting significant ARR growth for Agentforce and IBM focusing on its "agentic control plane" for broader enterprise applications. Kore.ai's strength lies in its proven, unified platform and superior analyst validation in the core conversational AI for CX space, allowing us to undercut competitors on platform maturity, predictable TCO, and independent validation.

## Watch for
- Continued analyst validation for Kore.ai and NiCE Cognigy as Leaders in Conversational AI for Customer Service.
- Any further clarification or shifts in Salesforce's Agentforce pricing models.
- IBM's progress in integrating third-party LLMs into watsonx Orchestrate and its impact on CX use cases.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven leadership in independent CX conversational AI evaluations** (Gartner, Forrester) to counter competitors' broad platform plays.
- Highlight the **predictability and transparency of Kore.ai's pricing** and TCO compared to consumption-based models like Salesforce's Agentforce.
- Arm Sales with messaging on Kore.ai's **unified, voice-native, and truly omnichannel platform** versus competitors with back-office origins or voice-first limitations.
- Reinforce the **compile-time validation** of ABL as a key differentiator for enterprise-grade reliability and governance, especially against less structured development approaches.
