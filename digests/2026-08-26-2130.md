## Watch for
- Continued integration and customer adoption of Salesforce's Agentforce in contact center scenarios.
- ServiceNow's progress in unifying its agentic AI offerings under the "Otto" umbrella and its impact on CX.
- Any further analyst validation or shifts in the competitive landscape for agentic AI platforms.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify Kore.ai's analyst leadership:** Continue to highlight our #1 and #2 placements in Gartner and Forrester's CX-specific conversational AI evaluations as a key differentiator against incumbents and big tech.
- **Reinforce ABL's TCO advantage:** Emphasize Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing and ABL's compile-time validation to counter the cost unpredictability and potential for errors in competitors' LLM-centric approaches.
- **Target voice-first gaps:** Position Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities against voice-only or voice-first solutions, highlighting seamless transitions and unified customer journeys.
- **Arm Sales with pricing traps:** Equip sales teams to probe competitors on their AI consumption models and the potential for cost overruns, contrasting with Kore.ai's transparent and scalable pricing.
- **Monitor partner ecosystem plays:** Track how competitors leverage partners (e.g., NICE with Route 101) and ensure our own partner strategy is robust and clearly communicated.
