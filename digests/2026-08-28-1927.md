# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Friday, 28 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI capabilities, with ServiceNow highlighting significant AI revenue growth and expanding state-level adoption, while Glean emphasizes its token-cost advantage and enterprise AI leadership. This underscores the market's rapid AI integration and the need for Kore.ai to continue emphasizing its platform's comprehensive, governed, and cost-predictable approach to agentic AI for CX.
