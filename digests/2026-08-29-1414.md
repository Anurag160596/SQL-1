*   Continued integration progress and product roadmap clarity for NICE Cognigy.

*   Emphasize Kore.ai's **proven analyst leadership** in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Forrester Wave, Gartner MQ) against competitors lacking this validation.
*   Highlight Kore.ai's **predictable TCO and cost-per-outcome model** versus the consumption-based pricing and potential overages of platforms like Salesforce Agentforce.
*   Reinforce Kore.ai's **native voice capabilities and true omnichannel experience** as a key differentiator against voice-first or voice-limited solutions.
*   Leverage ServiceNow's vulnerability disclosures to underscore Kore.ai's **robust governance, security, and compile-time validation** for enterprise-grade reliability.
*   Position Kore.ai as the **unified, enterprise-grade agentic platform** versus fragmented or bolt-on solutions from acquired entities like NICE Cognigy.
