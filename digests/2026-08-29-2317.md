# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 29 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, with Glean and Sierra highlighting significant funding and valuation increases, while ServiceNow grapples with AI platform vulnerabilities. Kore.ai's analyst leadership and robust ABL framework remain key differentiators against these evolving market dynamics.

## Three signals that matter

### Glean's Global Partner Push Signals Broader Enterprise AI Ambitions
**What happened —** Glean is outlining global growth strategies for its partners, emphasizing its enterprise AI ecosystem and the potential of GleanGO to "define enterprise AI" qz.com and IT Europa. The company also launched "Tau" to automate work across files, apps, and code HPCwire.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** Glean's expansion into partner-led global growth and its "Tau" automation tool indicate a move beyond pure enterprise search towards broader AI-driven workflows. Kore.ai's established global partner network and its comprehensive, CX-native platform offer a more integrated and proven solution for enterprise-wide automation, especially in customer-facing scenarios where Glean lacks voice and direct CX capabilities.

### ServiceNow Faces Critical AI Platform Vulnerabilities
**What happened —** ServiceNow has patched multiple critical vulnerabilities in its Now Platform and AI Platform, including three CVSS 10.0 flaws that put enterprise data at risk Techzine Global and csoonline.com.
**What it means for Kore.ai —** These critical vulnerabilities highlight potential weaknesses in ServiceNow's AI platform security and reliability, which are paramount for enterprise CX. Kore.ai's commitment to ABL's compile-time validation and robust governance provides a more secure and deterministic foundation for critical customer interactions, directly countering the risk associated with platform-level exploits.
