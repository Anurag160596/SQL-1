- Continued scrutiny of ServiceNow's platform security and its impact on enterprise trust.
- Salesforce's long-term pricing strategy for Agentforce and its impact on customer adoption.

- Arm Sales with messaging that contrasts Kore.ai's proven platform security and deterministic AI with ServiceNow's recent vulnerabilities.
- Develop TCO comparison tools that highlight the long-term cost predictability of Kore.ai's ABL-driven model versus Salesforce's volatile credit-based or per-user pricing.
