# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Sunday, 30 August 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are pushing their agentic AI narratives, but significant gaps in enterprise-grade reliability, true omnichannel capabilities, and predictable TCO persist. Kore.ai's ABL and analyst validation remain key differentiators against these emerging threats.

**What it means for Kore.ai —** This highlights the inherent risks in complex, rapidly evolving AI platforms and underscores the value of Kore.ai's compile-time validation and deterministic execution via ABL, offering superior reliability and governance for enterprise data.

### Salesforce Agentforce Sees Rapid ARR Growth Amidst Pricing Revisions
**What happened —** Salesforce reported Agentforce ARR of $1.2B, a 205% YoY increase, driven by a surge in deals, though the platform has undergone multiple pricing model changes in the past 18 months, most recently to per-user SKUs and flex credits, as detailed on (2026-08-29) by [Startup Fortune](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipwFBVV95cUxOby1sMjZQM1cxQmFzWmpoWVBBdVc4eF9nVHZ5UDNpUXc1bGVDS1owM09UVXN2d056R3g3ODRXbG5JTlJzVER2cUZGZGVXY0RoODhUMTdXYmhkbXZxV2lsd1dlUFBEVjlNUnQ5VlMxMmo2S05nMmRfQ1JfLVBWa2ZzYWl2RkNJYXk2Yk9wZzRZMFFGRmFGNk1VRGFvU0NreHlibmpLLTBGOA?oc=5).
**What it means for Kore.ai —** The continued pricing volatility and complexity of Agentforce's consumption-based models present an opportunity to emphasize Kore.ai's transparent, predictable per-outcome pricing and robust cost attribution capabilities, directly addressing CIO concerns about unpredictable AI spend.

## Watch for
- Continued analyst evaluations of agentic platforms, particularly for CX-specific use cases.
- Any further evidence of ServiceNow's ability to secure its AI platform against critical vulnerabilities.
- Salesforce's sustained ability to maintain Agentforce growth amidst ongoing pricing adjustments.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify messaging** on Kore.ai's ABL compile-time validation and deterministic execution as a critical differentiator for enterprise-grade AI reliability and governance.
- **Arm Sales with talking points** that highlight the unpredictable TCO and pricing complexity of consumption-based models like Salesforce's Agentforce, contrasting it with Kore.ai's predictable, outcome-based pricing.
- **Reinforce Kore.ai's analyst leadership** in Conversational AI for Customer Service (Forrester Wave, Gartner MQ) to counter competitors' broad "enterprise AI" claims without specific CX validation.
- **Develop content** that clearly articulates the distinction between employee-facing AI (like Glean) and true customer-facing, omnichannel CX AI platforms.
