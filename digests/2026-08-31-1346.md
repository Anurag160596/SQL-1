## Watch for
- Salesforce's continued integration of third-party LLMs and its impact on Agentforce's core offering and pricing.
- ServiceNow's ability to maintain platform stability and security amidst rapid expansion.
- Glean's success in translating its partner program into tangible enterprise CX wins.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **compile-time validation** and **deterministic execution** in messaging to counter the perceived risks of external LLM integrations and platform vulnerabilities.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **true omnichannel capabilities** (voice and digital) and robust back-end integrations as a key differentiator against Glean's more internally focused approach.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's **proven analyst leadership** in Conversational AI for Customer Service to counter competitors whose strengths lie in adjacent markets (e.g., CRM, ITSM, enterprise search).
- Arm sales with specific questions around **cost predictability and governance** for competitors leveraging consumption-based or complex pricing models.
