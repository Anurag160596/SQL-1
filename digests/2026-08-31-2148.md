## Watch for
- ServiceNow's continued integration of Moveworks into its "Otto" platform and its impact on enterprise AI agent offerings.
- Salesforce's ongoing efforts to clarify and stabilize Agentforce pricing models.
- Further analyst evaluations that differentiate pure-play CX AI platforms from horizontal AI solutions.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's leadership in independent CX conversational AI analyst evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) to counter competitor claims of market leadership.
- Highlight the predictable TCO and cost-per-outcome benefits of Kore.ai's platform, contrasting it with the consumption-based pricing models of competitors like Salesforce and ServiceNow.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities, particularly its voice-native features, to differentiate from voice-first or employee-focused AI solutions.
- Arm sales teams with specific questions about competitors' pricing models, integration dependencies (especially for voice), and their standing in CX-specific analyst reports.
