> **Bottom line:** Competitors are increasingly highlighting AI agent capabilities and broader enterprise AI platforms, but often struggle with true omnichannel depth, predictable TCO, and independent CX analyst validation, creating clear openings for Kore.ai's proven, integrated platform.

- Continued analyst validation for Kore.ai as a Leader in Conversational AI for Customer Service.

- Emphasize Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities and voice-native features against horizontal AI platforms like Glean.
- Highlight Kore.ai's independent analyst leadership in Conversational AI for Customer Service to counter platform vendors expanding into CX.
