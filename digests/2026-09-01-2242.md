# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Tuesday, 01 September 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing agentic AI capabilities, with Salesforce Agentforce and ServiceNow highlighting significant ARR growth and new platform features. However, both continue to exhibit pricing unpredictability and platform lock-in, creating clear openings for Kore.ai's transparent, model-agnostic, and analyst-validated platform. Glean's expansion into enterprise knowledge management, while not directly CX-focused, signals a broader trend of AI platforms consolidating their offerings, which could eventually impact the broader enterprise AI landscape.

## Watch for
- Continued emphasis on "agentic AI" and "AI agents" across all vendors, potentially blurring lines with traditional virtual agents.
- Glean's partner program success and any potential pivot towards customer-facing AI applications.
- Further consolidation or strategic partnerships in the agentic AI space as funding rounds continue.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify analyst validation:** Continuously highlight Kore.ai's Leader positions in Gartner and Forrester for Conversational AI for Customer Service to counter the narrative of broad market adoption by non-Leaders.
- **Sharpen TCO messaging:** Arm sales with clear comparisons on cost predictability and total cost of ownership against consumption-based and complex per-seat models from Salesforce and ServiceNow.
- **Emphasize true omnichannel:** Position Kore.ai's unified voice and digital platform as a critical differentiator against voice-first or digitally-focused competitors.
- **Leverage ABL's compile-time validation:** Continue to educate the market on the reliability and governance benefits of ABL, especially for regulated industries, contrasting with the probabilistic nature of LLM-driven agents.
- **Monitor Glean's strategy:** Track Glean's partner program for any signs of expansion into customer-facing AI, preparing competitive positioning if they enter the CX space.
