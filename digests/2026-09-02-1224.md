## Watch for
- Genesys's continued integration of AI capabilities and its potential impact on its established CCaaS leadership.
- Glean's expansion beyond employee-facing AI and any moves towards customer-facing contact center solutions.
- Continued analyst evaluations and market positioning shifts for agentic AI platforms.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's proven leadership in independent Conversational AI for Customer Service evaluations (Gartner, Forrester) to counter CCaaS-centric placements.
- Highlight the TCO predictability and deterministic execution of Kore.ai's ABL against competitors with consumption-based pricing and potential for unexpected overages.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's true omnichannel capabilities as a key differentiator against voice-first or digitally-limited platforms.
- Arm Sales with specific questions to probe competitors' pricing models and their impact on long-term cost of ownership as AI adoption scales.
