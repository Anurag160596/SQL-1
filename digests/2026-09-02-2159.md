# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Wednesday, 02 September 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing agentic AI capabilities, with Genesys and Salesforce highlighting their platform advancements. However, gaps remain in true omnichannel integration, cost predictability, and independent analyst validation, areas where Kore.ai's ABL and proven leadership provide a distinct advantage.

## Watch for
- Glean's continued expansion into enterprise AI search and agentic capabilities, and any potential pivot towards customer-facing applications.
- Continued emphasis from IBM on its "agentic control plane" strategy and its impact on their conversational AI positioning.

## Recommended actions
- **Amplify messaging** on ABL's compile-time validation and deterministic execution as a key differentiator against competitors pushing "full enterprise workflows" without sufficient governance proof.
- **Reinforce analyst validation** in all sales and marketing collateral, particularly highlighting Kore.ai's Leader position in the Gartner MQ and Forrester Wave for Conversational AI for Customer Service.
- **Arm Sales with specific questions** to uncover potential cost unpredictability and governance gaps in competitors' consumption-based or token-driven AI pricing models.
- **Monitor Glean closely** for any signs of expansion into customer-facing voice or omnichannel capabilities, which would make them a more direct competitor in the CX space.
