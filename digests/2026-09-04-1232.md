# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Friday, 04 September 2026)

> **Bottom line:** The market for agentic AI orchestration and underlying AI capabilities is intensifying, with Genesys launching new agentic features and expanding its ecosystem, while ServiceNow acquires an AI startup to bolster its platform. IBM is also pushing its agentic control plane to GA. Kore.ai must proactively highlight its mature, governed, and analyst-validated platform to differentiate against these moves.

## Three signals that matter

### Genesys Deepens Agentic Orchestration for CX
**What happened —** Genesys announced the launch of its AI Control Plane for CX orchestration and new agentic orchestration features, designed to manage AI customer service agents more effectively. (2026-09-02) [CMSWire], (2026-09-02) [No Jitter], (2026-09-02) [Techzine Global], (2026-09-03) [TechTarget]. Concurrently, Genesys expanded its AI ecosystem through new partnerships, including Adobe, Meta, and ElevenLabs. (2026-09-03) [Techzine Global].
**What it means for Kore.ai —** This is a direct competitive move into Kore.ai's core strength of platform-level agent orchestration and governance. Kore.ai should emphasize its battle-tested Agent Behavior Language (ABL) for compile-time validation and deterministic execution, offering a proven track record compared to Genesys's newer agentic autonomy. The ElevenLabs partnership highlights voice capabilities, where Kore.ai's native voice-first (SSML, barge-in, sub-500ms) and true omnichannel stack provides a robust counter.

### ServiceNow Acquires AI Startup Sweep
**What happened —** ServiceNow acquired Israeli AI startup Sweep in a deal estimated at hundreds of millions of dollars, with the aim of enhancing its overall AI capabilities. (2026-09-04) [Tech in Asia], (2026-09-03) [calcalistech.com].
**What it means for Kore.ai —** ServiceNow is aggressively investing in AI talent and technology, signaling a broader ambition for its Agent Platform and Otto. While ServiceNow's heritage is ITSM-centric, this acquisition could strengthen its ability to deliver more sophisticated AI agents. Kore.ai can leverage its purpose-built CX focus and proven analyst leadership in conversational AI for customer service, a category where ServiceNow is not recognized as a Leader.

### IBM watsonx Orchestrate Reaches General Availability
**What happened —** IBM announced the General Availability (GA) of new capabilities within watsonx Orchestrate, including cross-platform agent discovery, custom evaluation, and an AgentOps Agent. (2026-09-03) [IBM].
**What it means for Kore.ai —** IBM is solidifying its "agentic control plane" narrative, which directly competes with Kore.ai's comprehensive platform approach to agent management. Kore.ai should highlight IBM's recent slip to "Challenger" in Gartner's Conversational AI Magic Quadrant and emphasize Kore.ai's own Leader status, showcasing ABL's deterministic execution and clear CX-centric stack against IBM's back-office orientation and "confusing overlaps" between its AI offerings.

## Watch for
*   Further details on ServiceNow's integration of Sweep's AI capabilities and its impact on their Agent Platform roadmap.
*   Genesys's specific production metrics and customer adoption for its new agentic orchestration features and AI Control Plane.
*   IBM's efforts to clarify the distinction and roadmap between watsonx Orchestrate and watsonx Assistant for CX buyers.

## Recommended actions
*   Arm sales teams with battlecards emphasizing Kore.ai's ABL for deterministic, auditable agent behavior and compile-time validation, directly countering Genesys's new orchestration claims.
*   Reinforce Kore.ai's analyst leadership in *conversational AI for CX* (Gartner MQ, Forrester Wave) to differentiate from ServiceNow's ITSM-centric AI and IBM's Challenger status.
*   Develop messaging that highlights Kore.ai's true omnichannel and native voice capabilities, especially against Genesys's new ElevenLabs partnership, to showcase comprehensive CX coverage.
*   Create competitive plays that contrast Kore.ai's predictable cost-per-outcome models with the consumption-based (tokens, assists, credits) pricing of big-tech platforms (IBM, Salesforce, ServiceNow).
