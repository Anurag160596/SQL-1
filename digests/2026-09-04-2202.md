## Watch for
- Continued integration efforts by Genesys with Salesforce and ServiceNow, and potential roadmap conflicts.
- ServiceNow's ability to seamlessly integrate the Sweep acquisition and demonstrate tangible CX value beyond ITSM.
- Salesforce's long-term strategy for Agentforce pricing and its ability to gain traction in true omnichannel CX beyond its CRM base.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **unified, AI-native platform** and its ability to deliver true omnichannel CX across voice and digital, contrasting with competitors' fragmented or ecosystem-dependent approaches.
- Highlight Kore.ai's **predictable, outcome-based pricing** and the robustness of ABL for compile-time validation and deterministic execution, directly countering concerns around credit-based models and potential cost overruns.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's **leadership in independent CX analyst evaluations** (Gartner, Forrester) as proof of platform maturity and enterprise readiness, especially against competitors lacking this validation.
- Arm sales with specific questions around competitors' **integration strategies, cost predictability for scaled AI, and true omnichannel capabilities** to expose their weaknesses.
