# Competitive Signal Report — Enterprise AI Platform (Saturday, 05 September 2026)

> **Bottom line:** Competitors are aggressively pushing AI agent capabilities, with a focus on integration and expanding use cases. Genesys's acquisition of LivePerson signals a move towards consolidating omnichannel AI capabilities, while Salesforce and ServiceNow continue to emphasize their platform-centric approach to agentic AI. Kore.ai's strength in true omnichannel and its proven ABL framework remain key differentiators against these evolving strategies.

- Continued integration of LivePerson's capabilities into Genesys's broader platform.
- Further announcements from Salesforce regarding Agentforce pricing and adoption initiatives.
- ServiceNow's continued expansion of its agentic AI capabilities across various enterprise functions.

## Recommended actions
- Emphasize Kore.ai's **unified, true omnichannel platform** in all customer conversations, contrasting it with acquisitions and bolt-on solutions.
- Highlight the **predictability and transparency of Kore.ai's ABL framework** for cost and performance, countering competitor pricing uncertainties and credit-based models.
- Reinforce Kore.ai's **leadership in independent analyst evaluations** for Conversational AI for Customer Service, particularly against platform vendors with strong but separate analyst placements.
- Arm sales with specific talking points on how Kore.ai's **compile-time validation** prevents production issues, a key differentiator against less mature agent-building approaches.
